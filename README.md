# Superstore Sales Dashboard — Sales and Profit Analysis Using Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Superstore sales data from 2014 to 2017 using Microsoft Power BI. The interactive dashboard helps understand sales performance, profitability, regional trends, product performance, and the impact of discounts on profit.

## Project Details
- **Project Title:** Superstore Sales Dashboard — Sales and Profit Analysis Using Power BI
- **Course:** T.Y. B.Sc. Data Science
- **College:** SIES College of Arts, Science and Commerce, Sion West, Mumbai
- **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset (2014–2017)
- **Dataset Size:** 9,994 rows and 21 columns

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing states, regions, and product sub-categories.
- Examine monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Compare current sales with the previous year's performance.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Provide data-driven recommendations for business improvement.

## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query for data cleaning and transformation
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculated measures
- Data visualization and interactive dashboard design

## Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, and Total Orders.
- **Sales Trend Analysis:** Monthly sales compared with the previous year.
- **Regional Analysis:** Sales and profit comparisons across regions.
- **State-Level Analysis:** Identification of high-performing states.
- **Product Analysis:** Sales and profit by product sub-category.
- **Interactive Filters:** Slicers for year, region, and customer segment.

## Key Findings
- Total sales were approximately **$2.30 million**.
- Total profit was approximately **$286,400**.
- Overall profit margin was approximately **12.47%**.
- The dataset contained approximately **5,009 unique orders**.
- Sales increased from approximately **$484,247 in 2014** to **$733,215 in 2017**.
- California and New York were among the highest-sales states.
- The West region generated the highest profit among the four regions.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies were identified as loss-making sub-categories in the analysis.
- High discounts were associated with a substantial share of loss-making order lines.

*Note: Figures are approximate and should be verified against the final Power BI dashboard.*

## Business Recommendations
1. Review discount policies, particularly for products with low or negative profit margins.
2. Investigate pricing and cost structures for loss-making sub-categories.
3. Prepare inventory and promotional strategies for peak sales months.
4. Focus on profitable products and high-performing regions.
5. Monitor sales and profit regularly using interactive dashboards.
